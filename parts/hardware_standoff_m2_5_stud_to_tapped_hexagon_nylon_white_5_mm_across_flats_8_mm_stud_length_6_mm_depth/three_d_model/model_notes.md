# Model notes

Generic catalogue dimensions; not a manufacturer-specific drawing.

Model uses a 8 mm stud and a 5 mm blind tapped cavity with a flat floor. Tapping is limited to body depth minus 1 mm to retain a solid web; this is an illustrative assumption, not verified usable engagement.

Small edge and mouth chamfers are illustrative assumptions, not measured supplier features.

Full standoff models have helical basic M2.5 profiles; display models use smooth minor-diameter tapped bores and major-diameter studs.

STL is in millimetres. GLB is in metres with Y up. OpenSCAD is in millimetres.
