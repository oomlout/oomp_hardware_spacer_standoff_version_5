# Model notes

Generic catalogue dimensions; not a manufacturer-specific drawing.

M4 denotes screw compatibility; the plain clearance bore is 4.3 mm, not a thread. Outside diameter follows the user-specified catalogue series; supplier references support bore and depth choices only.

Small edge and mouth chamfers are illustrative assumptions, not measured supplier features.

Full standoff models have helical basic M3 profiles; display models use smooth minor-diameter tapped bores and major-diameter studs.

STL is in millimetres. GLB is in metres with Y up. OpenSCAD is in millimetres.
