# Model notes

Generic catalogue dimensions; not a manufacturer-specific drawing.

Model assumes a continuous M5 x 0.8 through thread. Supplier tapping depth and blind/through construction vary.

Small edge and mouth chamfers are illustrative assumptions, not measured supplier features.

Full standoff models have helical basic M5 profiles; display models use smooth minor-diameter tapped bores and major-diameter studs.

STL is in millimetres. GLB is in metres with Y up. OpenSCAD is in millimetres.
