# Model notes

Generic catalogue dimensions; not a manufacturer-specific drawing.

Model uses two 6 mm M4 x 0.7 studs and a solid hex body. The depth range is a catalogue extension; exact all-nylon supplier availability is not verified for every depth.

Small edge and mouth chamfers are illustrative assumptions, not measured supplier features.

Full standoff models have helical basic M4 profiles; display models use smooth minor-diameter tapped bores and major-diameter studs.

STL is in millimetres. GLB is in metres with Y up. OpenSCAD is in millimetres.
